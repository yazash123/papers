# papers/ index

Each PDF was identified by its content (title page, running heads, DOI), not by
its filename. In every case the content matched the filename's first author and
year. Two files needed a closer look:

* "Andreasson 2015 - Examining kinesin processivity…" is the eLife neck-linker
  length paper (Andreasson, Milic et al. 2015).
* "Andreasson 2015 - Mechanochemical cycle of mammalian kinesin-2 KIF3A-B under
  load [rates]" is the separate Current Biology kinesin-2 paper (Andreasson,
  Shastry, Hancock & Block 2015). Gicking et al. 2022 cite it as "Andreasson et
  al., 2015b".

**Not in the folder:** Kawaguchi & Ishiwata (2000), *Biochem. Biophys. Res.
Commun.* 272:895 (the temperature/stall study named in Part E). The file
"Kawaguchi 2008" is a later minireview by Kawaguchi alone.

Citation details come from the PDF itself (DOI, running head, or first page).
"n/p" means the PDF text does not print volume or pages, so they were not
verified here.

**Use** column: **A–E** = session parts where a paper supplied numbers or
checks; *read* = consulted for context only; blank = not used this session.
